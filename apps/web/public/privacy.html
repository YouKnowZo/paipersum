Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 20, 2025

Our Commitment: We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use PaiperSum.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Document Content: Text and files you upload for analysis
  • Account Information: Email address, name (if you create an account)
  • Payment Information: Processed securely by Stripe (we do not store card numbers)

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, time spent
  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system
  • Cookies: Session cookies for functionality (you can disable in browser)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and improve the Service
  • Analyze documents and generate reports
  • Process payments and manage accounts
  • Send service updates and communications
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Document Data Handling

Important: We do not store your document content permanently.
  • Processing: Documents are processed in real-time during analysis
  • Storage: Document content is deleted immediately after analysis (except for paid accounts that opt-in to history)
  • Analysis Results: We may store analysis metadata (risk scores, clause counts) for service improvement
  • No Human Review: Your documents are analyzed by automated systems only, not reviewed by humans

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Service Providers: Hosting (AWS/Google Cloud), payment processing (Stripe), analytics (limited)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect rights and safety
  • Business Transfers: In connection with mergers or acquisitions (with notice)

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • Encryption at rest for stored data
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Secure deletion of documents after processing

However, no system is 100% secure. Do not upload documents containing highly sensitive or confidential information unless you accept this risk.

6. Data Retention

  • Document Content: Deleted immediately after analysis (free tier)
  • Analysis Results: Retained for 90 days (or as configured in account settings)
  • Account Data: Retained while account is active, deleted 30 days after account closure
  • Legal Obligations: Some data may be retained longer if required by law

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications
  • Restrict Processing: Limit how we use your data

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@paipersum.com

8. Cookies

We use minimal cookies:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the Service to function (session management)
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand usage (you can opt-out)

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect functionality.

9. Third-Party Services

The Service may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Review their privacy policies separately.

10. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe we have collected such information, contact us immediately.

11. International Users

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. By using the Service, you consent to such transfers. We comply with applicable data protection laws.

12. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to know what personal information is collected
  • Right to know if personal information is sold or disclosed
  • Right to opt-out of sale (we do not sell personal information)
  • Right to deletion
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising CCPA rights

13. GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

For EU users, we comply with GDPR:

  • Legal basis for processing: Consent, contract performance, legitimate interests
  • Data Protection Officer: dpo@paipersum.com
  • Right to lodge a complaint with supervisory authority

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by email or prominent notice on the Service. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

15. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@paipersum.com
  • Data Subject Requests: dsar@paipersum.com
