Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 26, 2025

Your Privacy Matters: We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about data collection and use.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email, name (optional), payment information (via Stripe)
  • Documents: Text and files you upload for analysis
  • Communications: Support emails, feedback

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, analysis history
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information
  • Cookies: Session cookies, analytics cookies (see Cookie Policy)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide Service: Process documents, generate analysis, maintain your account
  • Improve Service: Analyze usage patterns, fix bugs, develop features
  • Communication: Send service updates, respond to inquiries, marketing (opt-out available)
  • Security: Detect fraud, prevent abuse, ensure system security
  • Legal: Comply with legal obligations, enforce terms

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Temporary Processing: Documents are processed in memory and not permanently stored unless you save them
  • Saved Analyses: Stored encrypted for 90 days (configurable)
  • Encryption: Data encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Access Control: Strict access controls, regular security audits
  • Backups: Encrypted backups stored securely

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Service Providers: Cloud hosting (AWS/Vercel), payment processing (Stripe), analytics (anonymous)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or to protect rights and safety
  • Business Transfers: In case of merger, acquisition, or asset sale

5. Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA Compliance)

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing emails
  • Restriction: Restrict processing in certain circumstances

To exercise these rights, contact: privacy@paipersum.com

6. Data Retention

  • Account Data: Retained while account is active, deleted 30 days after account closure
  • Analyses: Deleted after 90 days (or immediately if you request)
  • Usage Logs: Anonymized after 12 months

7. Cookies

We use cookies for:

  • Essential: Session management, authentication
  • Analytics: Anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics)
  • Marketing: Retargeting (only with consent)

You can control cookies through browser settings.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. International Data Transfers

Data may be transferred to and processed in the United States and other countries. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place (Standard Contractual Clauses, Privacy Shield successor frameworks).

10. Third-Party Links

Our Service may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

11. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance. Significant changes will be notified via email.

13. Specific State Privacy Rights

California Residents (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights including the right to know what personal information is collected, to opt-out of sale (we don't sell data), and non-discrimination.

EU Residents (GDPR)
