# PaiperSum Business Plan (Concise)

## Problem
Consumers and small businesses routinely sign contracts they don’t fully understand. Hidden fees, auto‑renewals, arbitration, and liability traps erode trust and cost money.

## Solution
PaiperSum provides instant, plain‑English insights into documents with clear recommendations (Sign/Negotiate/Walk Away), quantified impact, and negotiation templates.

## Product
- Core: upload/paste analysis, risk scoring, clause highlights, cost scenarios, negotiation templates, PDF export.
- Engagement: reminders for cancellations/renewals, shareable results, saved history, account dashboard.
- Premium: unlimited analyses, advanced reporting, family/team sharing, priority support.

## Market
- TAM: >1B online contracts/year. Early beachheads: fitness, telecom, SaaS, rental leases.
- ICPs: consumers (high‑intent events), prosumers (indie/freelancers), SMB admins.

## Business Model
- Free tier: limited monthly analyses to experience value.
- Pro: $20/mo (or $120/yr) unlimited analyses, reports, reminders, priority support.
- Teams: $15/seat/mo (min 3) with shared workspaces.

## Go‑To‑Market
- SEO pages by contract type; landing pages with examples.
- Creator partnerships (consumer finance, law adjacent).
- Browser extension for ToS scanning and lead capture.
- Referral loop in shareable results and PDF footers.

## Competition
- Legal tech readers and AI summarizers. Differentiation: clarity, quantified impact, concrete actions, delightful UX.

## Moat
- Opinionated recommendations + outcomes data feedback.
- Benchmarks by document type; pattern library of risky clauses.

## Roadmap (next 90 days)
- v1 polish, payments, shareable PDFs, examples library
- Extension MVP, reminder automations, Stripe billing + webhooks
- Teams, export API, templates gallery, outcome tracking

## KPIs
- WAU/MAU, first‑week retention, analyses/user, upgrade %, CAC:LTV, refund rate.

## Risks & Mitigations
- Legal risk: prominent disclaimers; no legal advice; escalation partners.
- Accuracy: conservative language; highlight confidence; human review option later.
- Data privacy: no storage by default; transparent controls.
