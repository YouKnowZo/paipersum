## ✅ NEW FEATURES IMPLEMENTED

### 1. Document-Specific AI Chat (`/api/chat`)
**What it does:** Ask questions about YOUR uploaded document

**Example:**
- User: "What are the cancellation terms?"
- ChatGPT: "Cancellation terms typically..."
- **PaiperSum**: "Your document's cancellation clause on page 2 says: 'You may cancel within 30 days by emailing support@company.com'"

**Implementation:**
- Uses semantic search to find relevant document sections
- Answers based on YOUR document, not generic knowledge
- Provides source citations linking back to original text

### 2. Visual Risk Highlighting (`/api/highlight`)
**What it does:** Highlights risks directly in your original document

**Features:**
- Color-coded highlights (red/yellow/green)
- Click highlights to see explanations
- Shows exact locations in document
- Works with PDF, DOCX, and text files

**Implementation:**
- Pattern matching for common risk clauses
- Context-aware highlighting
- Interactive viewer component

### 3. Document Comparison (`/api/compare`)
**What it does:** Compare two versions side-by-side

**Use cases:**
- Track changes in contract negotiations
- Compare old vs new terms of service
- See what changed between versions

**Implementation:**
- Upload two files
- Extract and compare text
- Highlight additions, removals, changes

### 4. Smart Notifications (`/api/notifications`)
**What it does:** Extract important dates and create calendar reminders

**Features:**
- Auto-renewal date detection
- Cancellation deadline reminders
- Payment date tracking
- iCalendar export for Google Calendar
