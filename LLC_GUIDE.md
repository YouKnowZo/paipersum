# LLC Setup Guide (US)

1) Choose state (often home state). Name availability search.
2) Registered agent (can be you or a service).
3) File Articles of Organization ($50–$300).
4) Get EIN (IRS, free online).
5) Operating Agreement (single‑member template). Keep personal and business separate.
6) Business bank account + Stripe/PayPal.
7) Licenses (if any). Keep compliance calendar (annual report/fee).
8) Liability basics: separate funds, sign as the LLC, not personally.

Consult a professional for your situation; this is informational only.
