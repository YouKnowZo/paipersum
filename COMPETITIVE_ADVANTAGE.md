# 🚀 PaiperSum Competitive Advantage Features

## What Makes PaiperSum Different from ChatGPT/DeepSeek/Google AI

### ❌ What They Do (Generic AI):
- Generic text input/output
- No document context awareness
- No specialized legal knowledge
- No actionable insights
- No risk scoring
- No cost calculations
- No browser integration
- No document-specific chat

### ✅ What PaiperSum Does (Specialized & Purpose-Built):
1. **Document-Specific AI Chat** - Ask questions about YOUR document
2. **Visual Document Viewer** - Highlight risks in original document
3. **Risk Scoring** - Quantified risk scores (0-100)
4. **Cost Impact** - Real dollar savings calculations
5. **Negotiation Templates** - Ready-to-send emails
6. **Browser Extension** - Automatic TOS detection
7. **Actionable Recommendations** - Sign/Negotiate/Walk Away
8. **Benchmarking** - Compare against industry standards
9. **Calendar Integration** - Remind about important dates
10. **Multi-Format Support** - PDF, DOCX, images, OCR
