# PaiperSum Business Plan 2025

## Executive Summary

**Company:** PaiperSum  
**Website:** https://paipersum.com  
**Mission:** Democratize legal document understanding through AI-powered analysis  
**Founded:** 2025  
**Founder:** Solo entrepreneur with AI assistance

### Value Proposition
PaiperSum provides professional-grade legal document analysis accessible to everyone. Our AI-powered platform delivers instant risk assessments, clause-by-clause breakdowns, and actionable insights for contracts, agreements, and legal documents.

## Market Analysis

### Target Market
- **Primary:** Individuals and small businesses (18-45 years old)
- **Secondary:** Freelancers, gig workers, renters, consumers
- **Market Size:** 50M+ Americans sign contracts annually without legal review
- **Pain Point:** Legal consultation costs $200-500/hour; most people can't afford it

### Competitive Landscape
- **Traditional:** Law firms ($200-500/hr), LegalZoom ($300+/document)
- **Digital:** Rocket Lawyer ($40/month), Lawdingo, ContractWorks
- **Our Advantage:** Instant, affordable, focused on consumer protection

### Market Opportunity
- Legal tech market: $28B+ globally (2025)
- Document analysis segment: $3.2B
- Growing at 12% annually
- 85% of consumers don't understand legal documents they sign

## Risk Analysis & Mitigation

### Risks

1. **Legal Liability**
   - **Risk:** User makes decision based on faulty analysis
   - **Mitigation:** Strong disclaimers, E&O insurance, accuracy testing

2. **AI Accuracy**
   - **Risk:** AI misses critical clauses
   - **Mitigation:** Human review samples, continuous model improvement, user feedback loop

3. **Competition**
   - **Risk:** Big players (LegalZoom) copy features
   - **Mitigation:** Speed, UX focus, community building, niche expertise

4. **Regulatory**
   - **Risk:** Classified as "practicing law"
   - **Mitigation:** Informational only, attorney partnerships, compliance review

5. **Cost Scaling**
   - **Risk:** AI costs grow faster than revenue
   - **Mitigation:** Model optimization, caching, tiered access, price increases
