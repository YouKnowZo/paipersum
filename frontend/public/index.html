Features

Your Smart Document Co-Pilot

PaiperSum helps you build, analyze, and protect resumes, contracts, and privacy docs.

Resume Builder

Instantly create, fill, and optimize your resume for any job. Upload, edit, and export as PDF.

T&C Analyzer

Scan privacy policies and contracts for hidden risks, compliance issues, and plain-English summaries.

Contract Simplifier

Upload any contract and get a clear, actionable summary with risk ratings and recommendations.

Upload & Reconstruct Your Resume

Upload your resume (PDF, DOCX, or image). Our AI will scan the content, font, and layout, then let you update info and rebuild your resume in the same style.

                            About PaiperSum
                        

                        

                            PaiperSum was born from a simple idea: legal documents should be understandable by everyone, not just lawyers. We're on a mission to democratize legal knowledge.
                        

                        

                            

                                

                                    
                                

                                

                                    
Our Mission

                                    
Protect users from unknowingly signing away their rights through complex contracts and opaque policies.

                                

                            

                             

                                

                                    
                                

                                

                                    
Our Vision

                                    
To be the world's most trusted legal assistant, making legal comprehension accessible and instant for everyone.
