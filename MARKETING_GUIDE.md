# Marketing Guide (Templates you can use)

## Positioning
- Headline: "Understand legal documents in plain English"
- Sub: "Instant insights, clear risks, real‑world next steps. Not legal advice."

## Landing page checklist
- Credibility markers (privacy, clarity, examples)
- Social proof (user counts, testimonials)
- Concrete outcomes (money/time saved)
- Strong CTA: "Analyze a document"

## Channels
- SEO: create pages for 20 high‑intent document types; embed example analyses.
- Social: weekly demo clips + case studies; creator collabs.
- Extension: “analyze this ToS” prompts; email capture.
- Email: onboarding + education sequences (see templates).

## Offers
- First‑analysis win: highlight saved money and avoided risks.
- Referral nudge: copy/paste share block with trackable link.

## Measurement
- Track: visitors → analysis start → analysis complete → account created → upgrade.
- Target early: 5% analyze, 50% complete, 15% account create, 3–5% upgrade.
